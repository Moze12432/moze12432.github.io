01About

Mukiibi Moses is a graduate researcher at Kwangwoon University specializing in artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data science, with research focused on emotion-aware AI systems, natural language processing, and the optimization of generative models.

He holds a bachelor's degree in computer engineering from Kyungdong University, and is currently pursuing a combined master's and PhD in computer engineering at Kwangwoon University, Seoul, South Korea.

His applied work spans sentiment analysis systems, text-to-image generation pipelines, and data analytics solutions built on AWS — aimed at intelligent systems that solve real problems at scale. He publishes and shares research through Google Scholar, ResearchGate, and Academia.
